## Open items for the operator (section 11 of the document)
- Service user group: physical, neuro- or mental health rehabilitation. This decides the ligature-resistance requirements.
- How the service is registered with CQC (Rehabilitation services or care home). This decides which fire regime applies.
- Whether the 19 m² rooms and 1,200 mm doors fit the existing building.
- Medical location group for the electrical installation.
- Hoist; nurse call manufacturer; sprinklers (ADB para 2.46); cooling; Employer-supplied items; planning and asbestos.
